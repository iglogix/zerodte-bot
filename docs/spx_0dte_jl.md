# 9:30am Jade Lizard Options Strategy Reference

## Overview

Sell 0DTE SPX jade lizard at the market open (9:30am)
Jade Lizard setup:
### Terms:
EM: expected move (calculated as the width of the strangle at 9:30am)
### Setup
Enter $30 wide put credit spread at 0.25x EM and $10 wide call credit spread

### Exit
Close winners @50% of the max profit (credit)

The loosers stay till expiration
